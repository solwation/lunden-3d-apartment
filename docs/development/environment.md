## The playhouse's secret children's room (#1319, part 1 #1326)

The red playhouse (`COURTYARD.playhouse`, #112/#681) is the way into a hidden children's room. E on its door (`courtyard.js`: an invisible pick box, target `kind: 'playhouse'`) goes through `main.js` `enterPlayroom()`: an avatar of `PLAYROOM.maxCm` (140) cm or more gets the badge "Du är för stor för lekstugan (under 140 cm)" (`avatarHeightCm(ghosts.myLook)`, #1127); a shorter one fades to black (`PLAYROOM.fadeMs`, one DOM element), `Player.spawn` puts them in the room and the fade comes back. The room (`src/playroom.js`, `Playroom`) is a separate hidden interior at `PLAYROOM.x/z` (-1500, -1500: outside `WALK`, inside the Worker's ±5000 m pose limit and further than `GHOSTS.far` from the playhouse), 6.4 x 5.2 x 2.4 m, built from a handful of merged meshes; its group is `visible = false` until you are inside (zero draw calls outside, 12 inside; `world.playroom.inside` is synced every frame, so a respawn or resume elsewhere undraws it). `Player` has an `inPlayroom` mode like `below`/`inCore`: its own floor (`groundAt`), four wall segments (`walkSegments`, `isFree`, `nearestFree`), ceiling, no obstacles, not `outdoors`/`aloft`, and the far-walk / slide / splash code skips it. `main.js` sets `day.under = 1` and `day.lit = PLAYROOM.lit` inside (no sun, an ambient lift) and names the place "Lekstugan · Barnrummet". The door in the south wall (`playroomExit` pick box) leads out again: `Playroom.outsideSpot`, `PLAYROOM.outside` m north of the playhouse's door wall facing away from it. A height change inside never throws you out (the door always works). A reload inside saves that outside spot (`placeNow`). Multiplayer: the ordinary pose stream already carries the room coordinates, so players in the room see each other and a player who goes in simply vanishes from the courtyard (too far for `GHOSTS.far`); `Ghost.push` treats a jump over `GHOSTS.jump` (100 m) as a teleport (no streak across the map). The room is empty: furniture #1327, billiards #1328/#1329, sound/TV #1330. All sizes are our own choices (*guess*). `tools/playroomtest.cjs` is the browser test.
