# #1752 fish in Höje å: result images

Two real clients (A "Alma", B "Bo") against the local Worker (`cloudflare/dev.mjs`), `tools/riverfishtest.cjs`, summer noon. The close-ups
are zoomed (field of view 28°) because a fish is 0.2–0.75 m long.

- `a-braxen-under-ytan.png`: client A on the bank, a bream under the surface (flattened, darkened shape over the opaque water).
- `b-samma-braxen.png`: client B on the opposite bank at the same moment, the same bream (same id, gap 6 mm in the test).
- `a-ring-pa-vattnet.png` / `b-samma-ring.png`: a roach has just risen; A sees the ring spreading from the fish (B's frame, taken a few
  seconds later, shows the same fish after the ring has faded). The test compared the ring's position at both: identical.
- `a-oversikt-med-spelare-b.png`: unzoomed view from A, a trout in mid-river and player B across on the other bank.
