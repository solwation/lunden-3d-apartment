# #1764: squirrels only change banks over the bridges

Top-down traces of the three squirrels over four simulated daytime hours in node (the real terrain, the river park's trees, the timber
footbridge and the gc bridge; seed 7; one dot per squirrel per second on the ground, bridge decks left out; blue = where `walkable` is false,
i.e. Höje å; brown lines = the bridges; green rings = trunks). Red dots are ground positions in the water.

- `squirrel-trace-valley.png`: the whole valley, before (231 dots in the water, 2316 of 228 033 samples at 10 Hz) and after (0 of 224 839).
- `squirrel-trace-bend-east.png`: close-up of the eastern bends, where the old runs and forage trips cut straight across the river.

Generated by a scratch script driving `src/squirrelbrain.js` exactly like the #1764 test in `tools/squirrelbraintest.mjs`
(`the real Höje å …`), which asserts zero samples in the water, no change of bank off a bridge and at least one bridge crossing.
