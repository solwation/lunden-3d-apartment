# Dust motes in low window sunlight (#2206)

Base: `8424540e`. `SUN_DUST` values are visual guesses, not measured scattering optics. The actual scene supplies its current `WindowLight` windows/region, `DayCycle.sunDir`, sun light intensity, room/level dust spots, blinds, curtains, walls and room-door segments.

The sun direction points towards the sun: `inSunPrism` projects a room point along it back onto the real glass rectangle. The sunlight volume runs from that glass along **−sunDir**. Motes outside its rectangle, room-side reveal, storey, region footprint, direct opening or unblocked wall/door path are rejected. Real drawn blinds mask the covered vertical part; drawn curtains mask the sides. This is analytic room-bound clipping, not a shadow-map sampler or full furniture volumetric shadowing; opaque scene surfaces still depth-test the transparent quads.

Only settled **dust** amounts in that window's own mapped room and level drive the count (`roomDust`); crumbs, smears, other rooms/storeys and unmapped/visited-flat sources do not borrow it. A clean room has no motes. The art-directed count saturates at dust amount 8. The whole batch has at most 200 quads, including at most two very faint narrow streaks. Levels 1/2 have 0.35/0.65 of the candidates; level 0 is hidden. `lowMemory` creates no geometry/material/batch. Low-sun height gating excludes night and high overhead sun; actual direct sun intensity scales the effect, not sky or lamp brightness.

The seeded small samples drift slowly and are re-seated at 5 Hz. Only a uniform clock twinkles them between CPU updates. One instanced mesh and one procedural shader, no texture, extra light, shadow caster, render target or new rendering pass. The two quad attributes plus matrix/plane buffers total about 16 KiB before driver overhead; seed and object overhead is additional. The material exists before normal startup shader warm-up, with the existing `warmDraw` marker.

## Cost and limits

Exact counts/checks/errors are in `browser.json`. All nine boolean runtime checks pass, with zero page/shader errors. Eight dust amounts produce 116 quads including one faint streak; one amount produces 13, clean produces zero. Both native views gained **one draw call and 232 triangles**: living 330→331 calls / 791234→791466 triangles, window 272→273 / 700425→700657. One new program is reused in the second view. After quality restoration settled, three more updates/renders kept **127 programs, 88 textures and 1417 geometries** unchanged. The projected transparent bounding-rectangle sum is **8204 pixels (1.34%)** in the room view and **33135 pixels (5.39%)** in the closer view. All four final native images were opened and inspected: small isolated grains are visible in the window prism, with no obvious wall/room spill, and the weak narrow streak does not wash over the room. This is representative inspection, not exhaustive furniture-shadow verification. The conservative overdraw estimate sums clipped screen bounding rectangles of **every** transparent quad, including their empty soft corners and overlap. It bounds blend-fragment coverage at the measured camera; it is neither a GPU timer nor a hardware measurement. The tiny dust sprites have a 1.1–2.3 pixel diameter cap; at most two streak cards use a 9 cm full width, with peak alpha 0.018 before sunlight/dust/falloff. No offscreen or full-screen postprocessing.

The browser wrapper used its shared slots but reports unavailable systemd memory scopes on this host. Physical **Iris 640**, mobile GPU overdraw and hardware FPS were **not tested**. Representative native images, exact draw/triangle additions and a projected coverage bound are the available evidence; no hardware performance guarantee is claimed.

## Public applicability

Inspected public `AGENTS.md`, `docs/upstream.md`, `src/main.js`, `world.js` and its config. Its actual app has fixed noon light (`world.windowLights.update(12, 0, true)`), no `WindowLight` indoor region/controller, no day-cycle `sunDir` and no life-simulation `Mess` dust state. This low-sun/dust-driven consumer therefore has no direct counterpart there. Do not import the original's life simulation or personal room furnishings to create one. The neutral prism/count helpers are reusable if a later public daylight feature supplies a justified neutral consumer; no active public port is necessary for this change.
