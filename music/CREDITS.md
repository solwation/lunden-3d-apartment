# Music credits

All twelve recordings are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The licence was checked on each creator’s source page, and the audio downloaded on **2026-10-07**. No attribution is required by CC0; we credit the musicians here and on the in-game music page anyway.

Each basename below has an Opus `.ogg` (64 kbps) and an `.mp3` (64 kbps) fallback. These are mono and loudness-normalised. Ten recordings retain their full length; `basically-not-fusion-jazz` and `background-space-track` are 120-second excerpts with a two-second fade at the end. Repeated clips may have a brief musical pause at the loop boundary. Source download URLs and original SHA-256 hashes are in `sources.json`. The background-space-track archive contains `MyVeryOwnDeadShip.ogg`; that is the recording used.

Files are streamed only when their source plays, into the existing Web Audio master and positional graph. Failed formats try the alternative, then generated music; stalled requests time out after eight seconds. Sound effects remain synthesised.
