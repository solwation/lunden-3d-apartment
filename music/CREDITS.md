Josh Woodward's seven recordings use his **instrumental mixes**. Kevin MacLeod and Scott Buckley use their full mixes. Each recording retains stereo and its full musical arrangement; it is loudness-normalised to −18 LUFS with a −2 dBTP target and encoded as **Opus 96 kbps** (`.ogg`) and **MP3 128 kbps** for format compatibility. No excerpt, synthetic instrument replacement or remix was made. Files stream only when played. Failed formats try the alternative; Sonos/radio also try the other recording in the same channel once, then display a load status and stay quiet. PC/laptop/Kaffeturbo remain quiet on unavailable media. Sound effects remain synthesized.
