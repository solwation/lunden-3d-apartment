# 3D Model Attributions

These are project-authored exports unless a row states otherwise. The original
migration issue numbers describe provenance, not a general rendering, loading or
memory improvement. Current consumers may retain procedural fallbacks and apply
their own materials/poses. Raw GLB mesh counts and file bytes are not submitted
draw counts, total GPU memory or hardware frame-rate measurements. The earlier cat
performance recommendation is superseded by
[the source-bound correction](../docs/development/rigged-cat-evaluation.md).

- `cat-rigged.glb` (#1918 surfaces on the #1910 recipe, replacing #1892/#1894): Project-authored neutral articulated Mesh hierarchy exported from the established `src/cat.js` recipe by `tools/buildcatglb.mjs` (56,324 bytes). The legacy filename is retained; this is not the former Peiffer Innovations SkinnedMesh. Loading substitutes only matching immutable geometry buffers; original per-breed/kitten transforms, coat/eye materials and washing/walking/petting/eating/held/climbing poses remain authoritative. No personal Miele image/map or visitor hand is embedded. Authored tapered skull/torso, curved ears and recessed eyes share immutable buffers; chest/blaze use vertex colour on continuous surfaces. Current scoped fixture submits 22 cat mesh calls/2,672 triangles with open eyes, 20/2,512 closed; historical #1910 was 24/10,008 open. Generic GLB contains no personal coat map. No hardware frame-rate or total GPU-memory claim is made.
- `squirrel-rigged.glb` (#1920 surfaces on the #1908 restoration): Project-authored neutral squirrel exported from the shared `src/squirrels.js` recipe by `tools/buildsquirrelglb.mjs` (147,840 bytes). A continuous tapered trunk/head and closed, curved plume with shallow fur flow replace the older overlapping spheres/locks. The established 25 bones, seven tail joints and five clips (`idle`, `run`, `flick`, `climb`, `eat`) remain authoritative. Runtime near/far surfaces preserve all ten palettes, widths, scales and pale tail tips, using vertex colours without a fur texture. Current submitted cost is one draw and 3,062/1,228 triangles per near/far squirrel; historical #1908 was already one draw, 3,382/1,396 triangles and 229,824 asset bytes. No three-to-one draw-call or hardware frame-rate improvement is claimed. Retained fallback/morph buffers and the separately loaded neutral resource are accounted for in `docs/verification/1920/README.md`.
- `duck-rigged.glb` (#1896, corrected #1909, refined #1921): Project-authored mallard surface exported by `tools/buildduckglb.mjs`, currently 60,324 bytes. Central DUCK_SURFACE profiles provide tapered head/bill, continuous breast/neck overlap, folded wings, low tail and webbed feet, with restrained sex-specific vertex plumage. Live GLB, missing-asset fallback and collection use the same surface recipe: one material/draw and 1,312 triangles per bird. Existing 14 bones, 5 clips and exact neutral bill contact remain. No texture or personal image. Historical #1909 live forms were 960/896 male/female triangles; old fallback was 640. See `docs/verification/1921/README.md` for raw/retained costs and measurement limits.
- `avatar-rigged.glb` (#1899, corrected #1907, refined #1916): Project-authored neutral avatar exported from `src/avatar.js`'s shared per-look recipe, with Olof's correctly bound 15-bone armature and 6 standalone inspection clips (`idle`, `walk`, `run`, `crouch`, `wave`, `sit`). The current continuous limb/head/garment surfaces preserve the supported appearances, private vertex-color palette and gameplay IK, with one SkinnedMesh and no replacement texture atlas. The current asset is 2,044,944 bytes (the #1907 correction was 1,813,152 bytes); no loading, memory or frame-rate improvement is claimed. See `docs/verification/1916/README.md` for bounded runtime costs and verification limits.
- `pony-rigged.glb` (#1897, corrected #1906, refined #1919): Project-authored pony loft exported by `tools/buildponyglb.mjs`, currently 140,088 bytes. Rounded/interpolated torso, neck/head/muzzle, flush eyes/nostrils and tapered mane/tail surfaces retain the existing 22-bone armature, inverse binds, 5 clips (`idle`, `walk`, `graze`, `watch`, `pet`) and per-animal coat/star atlas. Authored normals are preserved across UV palette boundaries. One material/draw and 2,652 triangles per pony; two actual consumers share 120,632 bytes of native attribute/index views (historical #1906: 1,080 triangles and 52,856 bytes). Retained procedural fallback geometry and GLB download bytes are separate costs; no hardware frame-rate or total GPU-memory saving is claimed. No personal photo is embedded. See `docs/verification/1919/README.md` for comparable views and limits.
- `car-megane.glb` & `car-parked.glb` (#1898): Project-authored vehicle exports consumed by `src/carmodel.js`: Renault Mégane E-Tech with four opening door pivots, rotating wheels and the existing cabin/materials, plus parked-car geometry for the existing four instanced material groups in `streetlife.js`. Procedural fallback paths remain. Whole-model and actual-consumer review in [#1905](../docs/verification/1905/README.md) found no demonstrated shape regression; it does not establish a rendering, startup or memory improvement from the format change.
- `bicycle.glb` (#1901, frame corrected #1912): Project-authored Swedish city bicycle with a continuous low step-through frame, mudguards, carrier, spokes, pedals and tyres in near/far LODs. `src/streetlife.js` supplies the loaded geometry or its procedural fallback to bicycle consumers, including the rideable proxy. [#1912](../docs/verification/1912/README.md) records the corrected frame and actual placements; no measured startup, memory or frame-rate saving is claimed.
- `fish-generic.glb` & `shark.glb` (#1902): Optimerade lågpoly 3D-modeller för fiskar och den sällsynta hajen i Höje å (`riverfish.js`, `shark.js`). Inkluderar riggad fisk med 5-bens skelett (`Joint_Root`, `Joint_Head`, `Joint_Spine_1`, `Joint_Spine_2`, `Joint_Tail`) med simnings- och vilotrack (`swim`, `idle`), statisk mesh för snabb instansiering, samt hajens fena, skelettkadaver och kropp. Ersätter runtime-sfärkoppling och procedursammanslagning.
- `santa.glb` & `pingping.glb` (#1903): Optimerade lågpoly 3D-modeller för figurerna i lägenheten: Tomten i soffan (`santa.js`) med sammanslagen vertexfärgad kropp, kinder med rodnad, rörliga armar, sittande och sovande benposer samt glögglas; samt pingvinkudden Pingping i sängen (`pingping.js`) med superellipsoid kropp, UV-kartlagt ansikte, fenor och fötter. Ersätter runtime-geometrisammanslagning och superellipsoidberäkningar.
