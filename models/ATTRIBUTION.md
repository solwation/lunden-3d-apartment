# 3D Model Attributions

- `cat-rigged.glb` (#1892): Rigged and animated 3D low-poly cat model based on open-source mesh and armature by Peiffer Innovations, LLC (MIT License), customized with project-made ginger tabby texture and skeletal animations (idle, walk, attack, dead, die, dance). Used for architectural evaluation and prototype of skeletal cat models in Kv. Lunden.
- `squirrel-rigged.glb` (#1895): Optimized low-poly rigged squirrel model with 25-bone armature (spine, head, snout, ears, limbs, 7-joint curved bushy tail) and 5 skeletal animations (`idle`, `run`, `flick`, `climb`, `eat`). Replaces procedural sphere/tuft geometries with a single SkinnedMesh (3 → 1 draw calls per squirrel) and dynamic canvas fur texturing.
- `duck-rigged.glb` (#1896): Optimized low-poly rigged mallard duck model with 14-bone armature (body, neck, head, bill, wings, tail, legs, webbed feet) and 5 skeletal animations (`idle`, `swim`, `waddle`, `flap`, `eat`). Replaces procedural sphere clusters with a single SkinnedMesh and dynamic canvas feather texturing for both drake and hen.
- `avatar-rigged.glb` (#1899): Optimized humanoid low-poly rigged player avatar model with 15-bone armature matching Olof's skeleton hierarchy and 6 skeletal animations (`idle`, `walk`, `run`, `crouch`, `wave`, `sit`). Replaces procedural geometry assembly with a single SkinnedMesh and dynamic canvas texturing for modular appearance customization.
- `pony-rigged.glb` (#1897): Optimized low-poly rigged pony/horse model with 22-bone armature (spine, neck, head, snout, ears, 3-joint tail, 4 articulated legs and hooves) and 5 skeletal animations (`idle`, `walk`, `graze`, `watch`, `pet`). Replaces rigid multi-block mesh with modern SkinnedMesh deformation and dynamic canvas coat texturing (Zoey and Princess with forehead star).
- `car-megane.glb` & `car-parked.glb` (#1898): Optimized low-poly 3D vehicle models for Renault Mégane E-Tech (drivable with 4 opening door pivots, rotating wheels, cabin interior with OpenR screens, and PBR shading) and closed-body parked street cars (pre-baked trim, tyre and paint geometries for instancing in `streetlife.js`). Replaces heavy runtime procedural extrusions with optimized GLB assets.
- `bicycle.glb` (#1901): Optimerad svensk stadscykel i lågpoly (diamantram, stänkskärmar, pakethållare, ekrar, pedaler och däck med LOD Near och Far) för cykelställ och cykelförråd i `streetlife.js` samt cyklingsbart fordon i `player.js`. Ersätter runtime-procedursammanslagning av Three.js-primitiver med en ren GLB-resurs.
- `fish-generic.glb` & `shark.glb` (#1902): Optimerade lågpoly 3D-modeller för fiskar och den sällsynta hajen i Höje å (`riverfish.js`, `shark.js`). Inkluderar riggad fisk med 5-bens skelett (`Joint_Root`, `Joint_Head`, `Joint_Spine_1`, `Joint_Spine_2`, `Joint_Tail`) med simnings- och vilotrack (`swim`, `idle`), statisk mesh för snabb instansiering, samt hajens fena, skelettkadaver och kropp. Ersätter runtime-sfärkoppling och procedursammanslagning.
